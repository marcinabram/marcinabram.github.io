Teaching and mentoring

I have taught very different rooms: specialized graduate machine learning classes, an introductory physics lecture for 120 students, IBM engineers in internal training sessions, and client teams in workshops, both as part of enablement programs and as exploratory sessions to support go-to-market activities. I have also supervised PhD and master's students, and mentored interns and engineers.

Students

University of Southern California, 2020 to 2024

At USC I founded the Machine Learning for the Natural Sciences group. Each semester I offered research-oriented internships for students from physics, computer science, and the data science program. In numbers:

  • 30+

    students did research with me, each for at least a semester.

  • 3

    PhD students mentored to completion (three successfully defended theses).

  • 8

    capstone projects supervised.

  • 4 papers

    with students as co-authors: quantum emulators, federated learning, and two on language models and context (2023, 2024).

  • 3 awards

    to student teams in USC's DataFirst program: Best Project Achievement in two semesters and Best Data Science Insight in one. Another team placed second in the RMDS Lab data science competition.

  • Alumni

    now work at NVIDIA, Meta, IBM, Intel, and Verisk.

At IBM I supervised four interns working on applications and evaluation of language models, and co-supervised an MSc thesis on context-window limits and the evaluation of generated text.
