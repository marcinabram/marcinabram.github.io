Machine learning

2020 to 2026

  • Emergent inference-time semantic contamination via in-context priming M. Abram arXiv:2604.04043 (2026) Five culturally loaded numbers given as few-shot examples shift a capable model's answers to an unrelated prompt toward darker themes. A smaller model does not shift.
  • Toward evaluation frameworks for multi-agent scientific AI systems M. Abram arXiv:2603.26718 (2026) How to benchmark scientific agents when retrieval can pass for reasoning, training data is contaminated, and novel problems have no ground truth. Includes a dataset of new research ideas and interviews with researchers in quantum science.
  • Why does in-context learning fail sometimes? Evaluating in-context learning on open and closed questions X. Li, H. Tang, S. Chen, Z. Wang, R. Chen, M. Abram arXiv:2407.02028 (2024) A context closely aligned with the topic does not always help more than a loosely related one, especially for open, difficult, or novel questions.
  • Context matters: data-efficient augmentation of large language models for scientific applications X. Li, H. Tang, S. Chen, Z. Wang, A. Maravi, M. Abram arXiv:2312.07069 (2023) The relation between the relevance of a context and the quality of the answer is not linear.
  • Emulating quantum dynamics with neural networks via knowledge distillation Y. Yao, C. Cao, S. Haas, M. Agarwal, D. Khanna, M. Abram Frontiers in Materials 9, 1060744 (2023) An emulator trained on a curriculum of simple examples generalizes to wave packets and potentials it has not seen.
  • Inferring topological transitions in pattern-forming processes with self-supervised learning M. Abram, K. Burghardt, G. Ver Steeg, A. Galstyan, R. Dingreville npj Computational Materials 8, 205 (2022) The uncertainty of a network trained on the inverse problem marks where a material's microstructure changes regime, with no labels.
  • Performance weighting for robust federated learning against corrupted sources D. Stripelis, M. Abram, J. L. Ambite arXiv:2205.01184 (2022) Weighting each participant by measured performance keeps a federation useful when some participants train on corrupted labels.
  • Model-free real-time autonomous energy management for a residential multi-carrier energy system: a deep reinforcement learning approach Y. Ye, D. Qiu, J. Ward, M. Abram Proceedings of IJCAI 2020, pp. 339–346

Decentralized systems

2019 to 2026

  • Distributed computer system and method of operation thereof J. Ward, M. Abram, D. Honerkamp US Patent 12,749,011. Filed in June 2021 with a priority date of June 2020, granted on 29 September 2026. The application was published as US 2023/0316127 A1 (2023), EP 3929742 A1, and WO 2021/260517 A1 (2021) Collective learning without a central coordinator. Worker nodes are grouped into clusters, each cluster trains a model on its own ledger without sharing raw data, and a main ledger coordinates the whole.
  • Democratising blockchain: a minimal agency consensus model M. Abram, D. Galindo, D. Honerkamp, J. Ward, J.-M. Wong Presented at Tokenomics 2019, Paris. arXiv:2006.05390 (2020) A consensus protocol that combines a directed acyclic graph with a chain of blocks, so that blocks are built collectively and no round leader can reorder or withhold transactions.
